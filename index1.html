WARM-UP

Meet the 6 Expressions

Click each expression. Think about its meaning and when you might use it. You do not need to memorize the Korean translation. Focus on the situation.

find out discover or learn information
Example: I need to find out what time the movie starts.
set up prepare, arrange, or create something
Example: Let's set up the classroom for the party.
sort out solve or organize a problem
We need to sort out this problem.
pay attention to listen or look carefully
Please pay attention to the teacher.
come up with think of an idea or plan
She came up with a great idea.
carry out do or complete a plan, task, or experiment
Students carried out an experiment.
💡 Teacher tip: For a 30-student class, students can work in pairs. Give each pair one answer. Ask: “Why did you choose it?”
